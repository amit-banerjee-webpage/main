About

Amit Banerjee is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at South Asian University (SAU), an international university established by all SAARC Member States. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from National Tsing-Hua University, Hsinchu, Taiwan, in 2009, followed by two years of research experience at the SoC Technology Center, Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), Taiwan. Since 2011, he has been with the Department of Computer Science, South Asian University, New Delhi. His research contributions have been widely published in leading peer-reviewed journals and international conferences. He actively engages in scholarly service as a reviewer for reputed journals and as a member of technical program committees of international conferences. Dr Banerjee is a recipient of the 2025 IETE Brig. M. L. Anand Award and holds professional memberships as an IEEE senior member and a member of the ACM.
